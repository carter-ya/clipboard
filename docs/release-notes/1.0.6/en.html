Clipboard 1.0.6

What's new

  • Choose System, Light or Dark appearance and apply it immediately.
  • Hold ⌘ or hover to reveal keyboard shortcuts for type filters and the All/Pinned tabs.
  • The AI settings entry now has soft golden sparkles. It respects Reduce Motion and pauses when the window is hidden, minimized or fully covered.

Improvements

  • A more native macOS interface for history and preferences, with Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later and compatible materials on older systems.
  • Improved multi-display placement and compact-screen layouts: history opens on the pointer’s display, and the preferences window prefers the same display as history.
  • Reduced rendering work for hidden windows while preserving unsaved preferences edits during temporary hiding or minimization.

Fixes

  • Fixed search focus after hotkey activation and returning from preferences, and removed search placeholder flicker.
  • Fixed appearance selector width changes when revisiting settings pages.
  • Fixed the launch-at-login switch getting out of sync with system settings.
  • Fixed clicks on the AI tab’s label and padding, while preserving native Control-click behavior.

Downloads

  • Separate Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) downloads remain available, each with a SHA-256 file. Requires macOS 13 or later. Auto-update selects the matching architecture.
